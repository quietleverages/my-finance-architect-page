Digital Delivery Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

This policy explains how The Finance Architect is delivered to you.

100% Electronic Delivery

The Product is a digital download. Nothing is shipped, and there are no shipping fees or delivery delays.

Instant Access

Access is provided immediately once your payment is confirmed:

  • Your download links appear on the checkout success screen.
  • A confirmation email with your download links is also sent to the email address you used at checkout by Lemon Squeezy, our Merchant of Record.

The delivery includes the 93-page Master PDF Playbook, the 5 fill-in-the-blank worksheets, Appendix A (50 Books Mapped) and Appendix B (Glossary).

Didn't Receive Your Email?

  • Check your spam, junk and promotions folders.
  • Make sure you entered your email address correctly at checkout.
  • Use the download links on the checkout success screen or in your Lemon Squeezy receipt.
  • If you still cannot find your files, email quiet.leverages@gmail.com with your order number and we will resend them.

Proof of Delivery

Because delivery is electronic, our payment processor's order and delivery records serve as confirmation that the Product was delivered.
